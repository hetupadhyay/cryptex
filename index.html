                    About Cryptex
                    
        
                    Made by Het Nimeshkumar Upadhyay as a cybersecurity course project.
                    
      
                    
      
                    
        
                    
          
                    Most people only meet encryption as a padlock in the address bar. Classical ciphers are small enough to watch working, letter by letter, and every one of them fails in a way you can see. Learning why they break is the quickest way to understand what modern encryption has to get right.
                    
          
                    Cryptex is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no frameworks, no build step and no tracking. Every cipher, animation and attack runs in your browser, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.
