Privacy

Effective 29 October 2026

Cryptex has no accounts, cookies, analytics, ads or server of its own. Everything happens in your browser, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

What Cryptex doesn’t collect

There’s nothing to sign up for, and the site sets no cookies. It has no analytics, no tracking pixels, no ads, and no scripts or fonts from other sites: every file comes from the site itself. The ciphers, the animations and the attacks all run on your device, so the messages you type into the lab, the codebreaker’s desk or the intercepts never leave it.

What’s kept on your device

A few small things are saved in your browser’s local storage, so they’re still there next time. They stay on your device and are never sent anywhere:

  • whether you picked the Day or Night theme;
  • which intercepts you’ve cracked;
  • your daily intercept streak.

Your browser also keeps a copy of the site’s files, so it opens when you’re offline.

To clear all of it, delete this site’s data in your browser’s settings, usually under privacy, cookies and site data. A private window forgets everything when you close it.

Secret links

When you create a secret link, the ciphertext, and the key if you include it, go into the part of the link after the # sign, called the fragment. Browsers don’t send the fragment to servers, so your message never reaches the site’s host.

The link is still visible to everyone you share it with, to any app it passes through, like a chat app, and to your browser history. And since classical ciphers are weak, treat anything in a secret link as readable.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like most hosts, Vercel keeps standard request logs, such as your IP address, your browser type, the page you asked for and when, to run and protect its service. Those logs are handled under Vercel’s own privacy policy. Cryptex adds no tracking of its own.

Links to GitHub

Links to the source code and to report a problem take you to GitHub, which has its own privacy policy.
