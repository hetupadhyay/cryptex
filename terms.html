What Cryptex is

Cryptex is a free educational website about classical ciphers, made by Het Nimeshkumar Upadhyay as a cybersecurity course project. It shows how historical ciphers work and how each one was broken. These terms cover your use of the site. By using it, you agree to them.

Never use these ciphers for real secrets

Every cipher in the lab was broken long ago, and the site itself shows you how to break them. Don’t use them to protect passwords, personal details, money or anything else that matters. For real secrets, use modern, well-reviewed encryption, like the encryption already built into your browser, phone and messaging apps.
